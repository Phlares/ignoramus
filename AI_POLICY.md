# AI policy

AI tools are welcome here as tools. These rules apply to outside contributions.

- **Say when you used AI.** Name the tool and how much of the work it did, in
  the issue or pull request.
- **You own what you submit.** You must understand every line and be able to
  explain it without the tool. You are responsible for its correctness and for
  its license.
- **A human edits AI text.** Issues and comments can be drafted with AI, but
  read and trim them before you post. Short and specific beats long and generic.

I close contributions that break these rules.
