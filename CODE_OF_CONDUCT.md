# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in ignorarium to be free of harassment for everyone,
whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient with other people.
- Respect different opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility for your mistakes, apologize to those affected, and
  learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing private information of others, such as an address or an email,
  without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions,
and any place where a person represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainers through a private security advisory
(Security tab, "Report a vulnerability"), with "Conduct" in the title. The
maintainers review every report and keep the reporter's identity private.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that break this code. They may warn, temporarily ban or
permanently ban a person, depending on how serious the behavior is.
